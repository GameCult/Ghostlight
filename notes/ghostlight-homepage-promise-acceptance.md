# Ghostlight Dungeon Homepage Promise Acceptance

Updated: 2026-08-23

This is the adversarial player-test map for the promises published by
`GhostlightDungeon-site/content/index.md`. The player-visible proof boundary is
the typed `ghostlight.play` Eve surface. Operator projections and receipts may
diagnose a result, but they cannot substitute for a coherent player experience.

## Authority

- `SessionZeroKernel` owns the negotiated campaign brief until publication.
- `WorldKernel` owns every canonical world revision after publication.
- The Eve surface projects permitted state and submits typed commands. It does
  not own authentication, character identity, results, or world facts.
- Heimdall proves account possession and access. A retained entitlement is not
  a browser authentication credential.
- Model stages, retrieval, narration, and strategic cells propose or explain.
  They never commit.

## Test order

1. Authenticate through the Heimdall Eve plugin and resume the existing canary.
2. Run impossible-action, effect-ceiling, and stale-assessment pressure without
   leaving the current location.
3. Exercise a three-or-more-character public event and probe local knowledge.
4. Travel away and back, including a daemon restart between the two legs.
5. Advance strategic time, inspect permitted news, and confront an autonomous
   institution with a demand against its interests.
6. Compile a fresh obscure start through Session Zero and pressure evidence-gap
   handling from the player surface.
7. Build the live named-refugee dispersal and return arc under cell budgets 1,
   4, 8, and 32. The callback promise remains open until this path is visible to
   a player, not merely present in a fixture.

## Stop conditions

- Any stale, malformed, unauthorized, or failed model result mutates world or
  Session Zero state.
- A browser-supplied actor, member, account, or outcome claim is accepted as
  authority.
- A location, character, relationship, possession, secret, or topology edge is
  silently reconstructed instead of loaded from canonical state.
- An arena emits collective speech, knowledge, ownership, or action.
- A human-controlled actor is puppeted by strategic simulation.
- A private fact appears in another player's projection, public narration, or
  an unearned NPC response.
- The interface makes a refusal technically correct but unusably opaque. The
  product promise includes a satisfying game, not merely a validator saying no.
