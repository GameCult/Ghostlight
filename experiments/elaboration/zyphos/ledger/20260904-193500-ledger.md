# Ledger Pass — Contradiction Quorums

## Idea

Sa'ueia breeding grounds must import a minimum contradiction quorum of mutually incompatible memory symbionts, because a developing body learns a durable self only by negotiating ecological claims that cannot all be obeyed at once. Safe disagreement is therefore a scarce reproductive input controlled by mobile families and unruly partner species; a commons starved of it can still raise young, but their tissues overfit to local consensus, making illness, ecological relocation, and memetic capture into birth-class hazards.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Breeding grounds gain young whose tissues can distinguish compatibility from mere repetition; mobile families and autonomous donor ecologies gain reproductive leverage because genuine independent provenance cannot be manufactured locally. Families with wide route standing benefit first. Newly divided families, refugees, archive-damaged households, and route-confined families are pushed toward narrow quorums whose delayed medical and mobility costs can compound into birth class. Donor organisms spend viable cultures and accept alteration risk; carriers bear contamination, transport, and consent obligations; infants undergo bounded but real immune stress; and the commons spends partner standing that cannot be repaid after the developmental window closes. The Empire benefits when it can blockade true donors or sell an authored imitation of plurality.

## Rejected first ideas

- Memory-eating pallbearer colonies manufacture clean biomass for nurseries: too close to the counterroot embassy's memory-eating borers, merely moving their trophic service to another address.
- Candle roads auction finite corpse-processing and disease-filtering capacity between breeding grounds: a prior Ledger pass explicitly left that lead, and it remains the obvious rationing story.
- Lantern-tree light or glassback heat becomes nursery currency: earlier Ember and Ledger passes already made route light, stored heat, and eclipse reserves into political infrastructure.
