# Veil — The Unclosed Roost

## Idea

A small lantern-tree grove preserves particular nesting places for absent pollinators without requiring promises of return. Uneven news lets a continuing welcome become a rumor of expected return, while trees and couriers choose how much absence should reveal or reserve.

## Underdeveloped territory and evidence

The less-used owning subject is the lantern tree as a selective host. `Umbros-Facing Lantern Trees.md` establishes remembered nesting permissions, individual illumination, invitations and mourning, dependence on incoming reports, and pollinator loyalty; it leaves the ordinary continuation of particular relationships largely undeveloped. Its established cognitive scale supports access decisions and lasting obligations. It does not establish introspective grief, verbal explanations, or romanticized tree psychology.

## New claims versus established facts

Established and checked against current files:

- Lantern trees preserve nesting obligations in deep memory tissue; use colored pulses for invitations, closures, taxes, mourning, and warnings; receive pollen, courier, and fungal information; support pollinators and depend on their continued service.
- Threadwings have individual preferences, pair bonds, route memory, and deliberate choices over delivery. Their vanes are ribbonlike; nectar, preening, warm roosts, and dependable light support cooperation.
- Fungal roads exchange information with trees and remember traffic through bodily channels. They do not supply omniscient travel histories.
- Mutable memory does not make every tissue independently reflective or confer a common humanlike mind.

Proposed additions requiring admission:

- Particular trees can voluntarily extend nesting permission through repeated absence; Crooked Cup sustains one such preference despite alternative pollinator offers. The opportunity cost matters even though direct lighting expenditure is not quantified.
- Couriers at this grove learn to separate continued hospitality, observed survival, and intention to return. Their selective reports can convey an encounter without also delivering its route. This bounded semantic separation is a new local application of established delivery discretion, not an already documented protocol.
- Local tolerance of withheld destinations, rumors about empty nests, and the particular newcomer/absentee encounters are invented cultural and story details.

Metaphysical implications: none proposed beyond existing organism-scale memory and agency. Attachment is a situated interpretation of repeated choice, not established subjective experience. The tree cannot read intentions or extract a courier's complete life from pollen. No colony puzzle, new species, universal signal code, or independent sentient memory tissue is introduced.

## What life is like

Returning couriers can enjoy a known landing place and provider. Young travelers learn invitations through accompanying experienced ones. Trees differ in how long they reserve space; some prefer current partners. Crooked Cup's willingness to keep receiving a particular visitor has value before it becomes difficult. The newcomer wants a specific nesting place and keeps supplying pollen while trying to obtain it. No Sa'ueia spokesperson is required to make these choices legible.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Absent couriers gain a reliable option and some privacy. Trees may retain dependable pollinators. Newcomers lose access to desirable forks, and trees may forgo new nesting relationships. Couriers carrying partial news risk its misinterpretation. The costs are reserved space, continued signaling, and missed exchanges; there is no invented metabolic crisis or inevitable betrayal beneath the benefit.

## Hooks left

Disclosure of survival may prolong the reservation rather than end it. A new nesting partner may continue seeking the favored fork despite accepting another branch. A return visit may fulfill the welcome without restoring the old pattern of life.

## Rejected first ideas

A trusted burden flower replaying an old host-state could create a good privacy problem, but grooming, companion custody, and remembered disclosure already have substantial proposal coverage. Mirror-amoeba companionship would require inventing person-scale understanding explicitly denied by the owner. A lantern tree performing memorial light for an absent courier would prematurely equate missing with dead and assert grief beyond the evidence. The chosen practice preserves uncertainty about both the absentee and the tree while keeping their actions consequential.
