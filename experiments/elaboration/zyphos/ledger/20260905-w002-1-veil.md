# Veil — The Unanswered Scraps

## Idea

A small community of lattice-ant colonies exchanges staged, incomplete traces for the pleasure of reconstructing what happened. Their disagreement over when to reveal an answer becomes consequential when a courier mistakes demonstrated uncertainty for extraordinary forensic power.

## What life is like

The colonies clean and diagnose for sustenance, then spend some available effort on questions of their own choosing. They admire accuracy, quick reconstruction, ingenious alternative explanations, or keeping an investigation open, in differing proportions. Invitations reward a good puzzle. A demonstration gives neighbors a way to reveal an answer using bodily action and local evidence. There is no invented ant childhood schooling or independent worker caste culture.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Participating colonies gain stimulation, reciprocal access to puzzles, and potentially clients. Couriers can contribute material and see an event through another kind of perception. Participation uses diagnostic bodies, glue, and time otherwise available for food and brood support; well-provisioned colonies have more room for it. Premature disclosure can spoil a desired experience, while delayed disclosure can lose the last opportunity to verify. Inflated rumors invite misplaced reliance and can damage the colony's livelihood even when it did not originate them. Neither partner betrayal nor secret atrocity is needed.
