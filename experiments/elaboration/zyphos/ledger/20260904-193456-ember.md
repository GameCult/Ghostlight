# Ember Pass Ledger

## Idea

On Zyphos, quarantine can keep a body alive while killing its ecological identity: once enough trusted partners lose bidirectional contact, they open the silent body's niche to provisional heirs who inherit its work, debts, and access. The only way to prevent this "ecological probate" is to keep a witness corridor alive through containment, which means preserving the exact reciprocal traffic that blight, mirror amoebae, and imperial memetics can use to escape.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Dependent ecologies benefit because essential work can continue before a quarantined partner recovers or dies; provisional heirs gain access, standing, and territory; Matriarch sponsors and the Airawa Empire gain a weapon that can annex functions without openly annexing bodies. Quarantined parties, their dependents, loyal witnesses, and later returnees are harmed because survival no longer guarantees restoration and every successful replacement creates an interested opponent to reopening. Heirs must accept the predecessor's labor, debts, dependents, grievances, and contamination risk, while any witness corridor that preserves continuity also preserves a possible route for blight, mirror amoebae, or imperial memetics.

## Hooks left

- Can a witness corridor carry enough fresh, costly work to prove agency without carrying the contaminant that forced quarantine?
- What redress is possible when provisional heirs prefer a safe and profitable predecessor absence to reintegration?
- Can a fraudulent probate be challenged after every surviving witness has inherited access, nourishment, or status from the silence?

## Rejected first ideas

- Repeated emergency detours drive prismwake mats into coordinated exclusion: the previous Ember ledger names this safe neighbor directly.
- A sentient cordon species charges passage through quarantine: candle roads already own that behavior, so a new organism would only duplicate authority.
- Mirror amoebae develop a continent-scale identity plague: this adds a larger bug, not a new pressure generated by the world's existing relationships.
- Matriarchs starve rival lantern groves and seize their traffic: an ordinary resource siege too close to the prior lantern-blight and pollen-escrow work.
