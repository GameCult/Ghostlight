## What life is like

Families return to meals, compare food and construction skills, teach cuts that preserve viable colony tissue, and develop gardens suited to different journeys. Quick fresh crops and slow, resilient gardens are legitimate specializations. Skilled growers can aspire to fine traveling food, longer routes, or reliable local supply. A starter is a living agricultural input whose usefulness must survive actual changes in food, not merely a prestigious old possession.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Growers gain meals and a portable craft; reefs receive materials and channel maintenance subject to their own needs. Families with provisions and multiple accepted sites can preserve donor stock through lean intervals. New growers may consume that stock before its benefits arrive, or exchange labor and goods to bridge the interval. Starter gifts plus provisions can alter this material disadvantage without creating an office.

Harvest removes biomass and may narrow surviving digestive capacity; severe cuts also remove reproductive tissue and disrupt carrier-cell exchange. Unharvested tissue consumes the same limited food as young stock and reefs. Moving a starter entails live-water transport and compatibility risk. There is no free-memory subsidy or assumption that additional racks increase total food production without limit.
