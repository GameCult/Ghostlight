# Patina Pass Ledger

## Idea

On the Sa'ueia continent, respectable grooming preserves a deliberately ungroomed strip of living surface growth—the witness patina—which feeds on a traveler's sweat and shed symbionts and bargains independently with roads, mats, flowers, and breeding grounds over what it will attest. Because repeated movement has no central archive, stripping, sterilizing, or replacing that patina can hide a traveler from old pursuers but also leaves them ecologically unwitnessed, so the injured, displaced, quarantined, and private must buy accompaniment or accept that the continent will price their clean skin as uncertainty.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

- **Benefits:** Witness-patina colonies receive food, continuity, travel, and standing as parties rather than residue. Mobile families gain decentralized provenance; roads, mats, and breeding grounds gain coarse testimony without owning a traveler's private memory; burden flowers gain a paid grooming and interpretation niche.
- **Harmed:** Solitary or displaced travelers, patients whose treatment requires total decontamination, people hiding from coercive partners, and anyone whose patina contests their account bear more delay and suspicion than well-connected families able to assemble substitute witnesses.
- **Costs:** Hosts must spend daily grooming labor, mineral wash, and protected body or equipment space while tolerating bounded disclosure and occasional false alarm. Removing the patina purchases privacy or safety at the price of quarantine time, deposits, escorts, or companion testimony; keeping it means carrying a witness that can refuse cooperation.

## Hooks left

- When a witness patina contradicts its host or a portable archive, which relationship gets to call the disagreement illness, self-defense, or testimony?
- Which routes recognize substitute witnesses after necessary decontamination, and who gains power by becoming the customary escort for clean-skinned travelers?
- Can a hunted traveler persuade a patina to forget without killing or abandoning it, and what does the colony demand for surrendering the history that keeps it valuable?

## Rejected first ideas

- Family-specific burden-flower pruning styles were rejected as cheap runtime detail: the flower already records grooming patterns, and a prettier care sequence would add no pressure.
- Candle-road flavor vocabularies and carried starter pastes were rejected as safe neighbors of existing corpse chemistry, spore transport, and material payment.
- Brood-ground arrival postures and nursery scent etiquette were rejected because the caretaker-handover and reserve ledgers already occupy that ceremony; another courtesy would not change who can move or refuse.
- A licensed grooming caste was rejected as a familiar credential office wearing symbionts; it centralized authority that the mobile ecological mesh has no reason to surrender.
