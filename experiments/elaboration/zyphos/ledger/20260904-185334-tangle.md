# Tangle Pass Ledger

## Idea

Rival Matriarch Trees prove alliances by admitting a foreign-sponsored brood of memory-eating root borers as a living embassy inside their own outer archive roots. The brood keeps its host healthy by pruning infected tissue, but it reports what the host sacrifices, depends on both rivals to reproduce, and can turn one tree's immune survival into another tree's political leverage.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Allied Matriarchs gain a hard-to-fake health witness, a living channel for negotiation, and leverage over a rival's immune ecology; candle roads, threadwings, Airawa dependents, and the borer broods gain paid roles in maintaining the pact. The host loses sugars, some weakly stored archive tissue, and the freedom to conceal root sickness; local healers and constituencies whose memories are classified as expendable bear the worst cost. If either tree starves, edits, monopolizes, or exterminates the brood, both the host's infection control and the surrounding food-web alliance can fail together.

## Hooks left

- Can a counterroot brood refuse both sponsor and host, and what jurisdiction can recognize such a defection without treating a sentient embassy as stolen property?
- Which constituency gets to challenge a Matriarch's claim that the memories eaten during pruning were diseased rather than politically inconvenient?
- Can the Airawa Empire exploit a counterroot embassy as a memetic ingress without making the brood legible enough for its host to detect the attack?

- Disconnected networks recruit fungal roads after treaty failure: the previous Tangle ledger names this exact safe neighbor.
- A Matriarch starves a rival's lantern trees to seize eclipse traffic: an ordinary resource siege, and the Ember pass already made route-light collapse politically live.
