## What life is like

Anticipation, inaccurate predictions, repeated visits, and learning what another traveler wants give the association its appeal. Competence means timing a journey around unlike bodies and existing obligations. Members may travel briefly, organize contacts, or pursue the full visit. Their outward ambition is social recognition through a particular encounter, with curiosity and status both present.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Couriers gain opportunities to be welcomed for chosen interests; less prominent families gain direct relationships; sponsors can earn prestige; receiving groves may gain pollinator traffic and reports. A prominent family risks losing primacy and bearing responsibility without exclusive credit. Couriers risk reliable patronage by pressing a public claim. Additional travel costs time, food, and attention to cargo and nests; no universal surplus or right of passage is invented. Local hosts can decline. No party needs a concealed atrocity for the disagreement to matter.
