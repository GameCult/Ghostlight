# Tangle Pass Ledger

## Idea

Matriarch Trees can annex a rival's food web without moving a root: they cultivate a keystone mobile guild whose descendants can complete their diet only by crossing between both groves. The resulting Two-Crown Hunger gives the guild political leverage and makes peace materially self-enforcing, until either Matriarch changes her half of the ration and turns inherited dual citizenship into a cascading famine.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

The allied Matriarchs gain a self-enforcing corridor and a faction inside each other's food web; the hinge guild gains protected feeding, breeding access, and the ability to halt services to both trees; cross-crown Airawa, route bodies, producers, and scavengers gain a constituency organized around keeping the border permeable. Guild descendants inherit an appetite they did not choose, single-root constituencies lose local autonomy, and peripheral organisms pay first when pollination, carrion clearing, spore carriage, parasite pruning, or reports fail. Both groves spend scarce sugars, minerals, shelter, and reproductive access, admit foreign symbionts and disease paths, and let redundant service guilds decay; the deeper the bargain becomes, the more a missed ration can turn into a trophic cascade.

## Hooks left

- If a hinge guild finds a third ecology able to complete its diet, is that emancipation, treaty theft, or the founding of a new nation scale route body?
- When one Matriarch dies, what obliges a disputed successor to maintain her half-ration before the hinge guild's broods begin to fail?
- Can a hinge guild deliberately retune its own symbionts and reverse the dependency, making two Matriarchs compete to remain edible?

## Rejected first ideas

- Matriarchs embargo rival pollinator routes: pollen escrow already occupies reproductive route sanctions, and a plain embargo is the obvious neighbor.
- Disconnected networks recruit fungal roads and couriers after treaty failure: the earlier Tangle ledger names this exact lead.
- A foreign symbiont witnesses a rival's archive health from inside its roots: Counterroot Embassies already landed that organism, mechanism, and leverage.
- A Matriarch induces refugee migration by starving her own dependents: this remained a one-directional resource siege until the dependents became a consenting, inherited two-grove constituency.
