**The strongest ideas**

**Longbank Clay Walks — aetheria-hearth-20260905-w001-1.**  
Worth reading because touch changes what an artwork must do. A detailed sculpture can fail as an invitation; a crude hollow can remain important because someone tended it with you. The firing proposal is particularly good: one household sees preservation while a living contributor sees the end of unfinished work. The Enclave source supports memory, care, independent communities, and an Aya alliance. Longbank’s placement and livelihood remain clearly proposed, not retrieved history.

**Second Learners — delvehold-hearth-20260905-w001-1.**  
The grandmother’s puppet-company invitation is a whole story in a few lines. Her ambition, her grandson’s encouragement, and her son’s dependence can all remain sincere. This supplies orcish life with aspiration beyond conflict and gives older people futures. The source explicitly supports local dungeon agreements and reciprocal obligations; the candidate teaches specific observances without granting children author-level dungeon ontology. Precise geography remains open but does not prevent shortlisting.

**Lethmere Shorefolk — delvehold-patina-20260905-w001-4.**  
The seasonal movement produces two desirable forms of home. Privacy and crowded affection each have their season, so permanent housing could improve lives while weakening something residents still want. Pottery, room ownership, absent names in prayers, and painful knees arise naturally from that arrangement. Eventual placement needs suitable flooding and building conditions; the proposal already identifies those as local additions.

**Waking Houses — kalsa-hearth-20260905-w001-1.**  
This is the clearest example of a world condition becoming ordinary social experience. Courtship changes when someone is available, and availability changes care. The widowed cook’s household has become indispensable without anyone asking whether she wants that future. Spoker ancestry supplies underdeveloped territory without conferring ancient technical competence. The article is ready as an author-facing candidate; its workshop commentary is not a ready Public projection.

**Near Flights — zyphos-hearth-20260905-w001-1.**  
The activity can be rehabilitation, flirtation, recreation, or lingering attachment, depending on who flies. The old sequence altered around a new companion is especially promising because the disagreement happens through movement. Threadwing sources already establish individual preferences, pair bonds, regrowing vanes, and competing symbiont obligations. Sensorimotor readjustment remains a proposed biological consequence, not a discovered fact. That uncertainty is properly visible in the submitted text and ledger.

Two declined entries deserve retention as reserves: **The Long Garden** for friendship across unequal lived time, and **Second Visit Association** for public lives after rescue. Neither needs a manufactured contradiction to justify passing over it in this wave.

**Updated prose calibration**

Retain the concrete openings: carrying doors uphill, finding breakfast after waking, following a companion’s turn. They make the reader understand before being asked to admire.

The new recurring template is **custom → patient teacher → shared meal → unequal burden → departure dilemma**. Longbank, Ilenmere, Brindlebank, Clayfold, Guest Bloom, Fold Gardeners, and Near Flights all use substantial parts of it. Each can work individually; repetition makes unrelated cultures sound supervised by the same exemplary adult.

Specific adjustments:

- Let some admired skills involve daring, timing, accuracy, comic invention, or rivalry. Patient teaching need not be every society’s highest achievement.
- Reduce jokes whose entire movement is “elders complain, then secretly participate.” Keep jokes arising from particular relationships or work.
- Move sentences about what an “owning note” leaves undeveloped into ledgers. Clayfold and Hanging Suppers lose momentum when the workshop enters the room.
- Let wonder remain present for more than a sentence before accounting for its costs. Necessary limits can emerge when someone acts.
- Avoid declaring each side reasonable before showing the disagreement. A person can be mistaken, vain, evasive, or unfair without turning the culture into a trap.
- Resist deleting useful boundary sentences mechanically. The distinction between a pleasant reef pulse and permission to pass protects an actual story premise; it earns its place.
- Do not polish every ending into a statement of cultural meaning. An unfinished action often leaves more room for the reader.
