# Idea

Kalsan conspirators sometimes manufacture a **closed hour**: a brief interval in which every known road, spare, messenger, archive copy, relief worker, and decision right that could carry a rival intervention has been gathered under one command or made too late to matter. It is not an anti-magic field; because Kaos still needs reachable levers, seizing ordinary contingencies can make a murder, succession, relic activation, or engineered failure visible to prophets yet almost impossible for gods, offices, or witnesses to change without first committing an obvious crime.

# Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Succession factions, military commands, cult hierarchies, monopolist custodians, and anyone needing one irreversible act benefit from manufacturing a period in which every lawful correction answers to them. The immediate harms fall on the person to be seated, killed, displaced, bound, or blamed, then on workers, couriers, households, outer wards, and technical dependants whose relief, copies, roads, stores, and refusal rights were consumed to close the interval; would-be breakers must often damage public works or violate custody before they can help. Closure costs bribes, coercion, simultaneous orders, committed reserves, disabled redundancy, exposed conspirators, and extreme brittleness, while defenses cost duplicate routes, idle capacity, separate witnesses, slower handoffs, and people paid to remain independently able to say no.

- Can a Crown City ever be closed while unknown custodians, surviving machine processes, or inhabitants below the mapped galleries may still possess an uncounted lever?

# Rejected first ideas

- A sacrificial storm ward deliberately left weak — refused as a safe local extension of the Narrow Shield and the newly landed open margin.
- A decoy route published to crowd the true evacuation road — refused because the Closed Lift, Grain at Two Gates, forecast-disclosure rules, and Cinder Concord already own its machinery.
- A court preserving one controlled casualty so an averted prophecy remains provable — refused because the evidentiary incentive was sharp but no current owner justified making deliberate injury routine.
- Successors impersonating a dead intermediary to keep a god's attention — refused because it would settle binding and post-mortem cognition beyond current canon and drift into Numen/Charter territory.
