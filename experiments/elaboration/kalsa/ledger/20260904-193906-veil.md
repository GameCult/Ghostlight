# Veil — Tally Ghosts

## Idea

Some potential stewards secretly keep a thaumavore at a known fork in a miracle route because its feeding response is the only witness their office can preserve when a god or temple declares the reserve empty. A tally ghost can expose an off-ledger champion grant, but making that evidence legible diverts real miracles into the contained claimant and may advance the witness toward becoming the next divine claimant.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Household-minimum coalitions, contributor and severance networks, mortuary witnesses, and independent auditors gain a narrow way to show that undeclared flow crossed a prepared fork. Gods, champions, and stewards can also benefit by licensing one visible feeder as an accounting sink, blaming it for failed promises, starving it into silence, or routing around it; rivals can feed it to manufacture an accusation. The tally dead are sustained but kept hungry and available, denied claimants pay for every repeat test, and custodians, clerks, and carriers become targets when their separated records approach a complete route. The cost is an actual grant withdrawn from healing, burial, shelter, or defense, plus route preparation, independent witness, containment, support for dependants, the possible destruction of the ghost's present agency, and the risk that repeated evidence-making opens an ascent route.

## Hooks left

- When a tally ghost voluntarily releases after years of service, does its silence clear the hierarchy, invalidate old audits, or expose an office whose solvency depended on keeping one dead person from leaving?
- Can a tally ghost bargain for its descendants' place in the household minimum without making every later answer purchased testimony?
- If undocumented grants give a tally ghost independent agency, who has standing to close the route, and which suppressed claims become the first obligations of a possible new god?

## Rejected first ideas

- Nameless precedents whose restored identities reopen old futures — refused as a safe extension of the Closed Lift's anonymized copies and the Cinder Concord's distributed archive danger.
- Civic offices that seed contradictory rumors to keep horizons crowded — refused as generic information control without a sufficiently material witness or cost.
- Gods using rival dead as deniable diplomatic channels — refused because canon does not establish reliable control or identity of a manifestation.
- A court declaring a god insolvent after failed miracles — refused as the obvious legal extension of potential audit, with no new pressure beyond existing offices.
