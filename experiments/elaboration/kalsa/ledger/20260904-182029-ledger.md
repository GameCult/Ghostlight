# Ledger — The Open Margin

## Idea

Several Ju'onai Measured Offices maintain an **open margin**: land, routes, buildings, and work schedules deliberately kept reversible so Analysis, Prophecy, or a divine intervention still has a material hinge when a crowded crisis closes the obvious plan. Patrons can pay to replace their margin elsewhere, so client wards and insecure tenants become the empire's stored alternatives—the road that may be cut, the field that may be flooded, and the household kept movable because someone else's miracle may need their future.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Measured Offices, public-work operators, crisis planners, cults, and the people protected by a still-reachable alternate gain real resilience when forecasts crowd or the primary configuration fails. Client wards, insecure tenants, low-expression workers, small growers, and households outside a patron's relief rolls bear shortened tenure, forbidden improvements, displacement readiness, lost wages, and the risk that their district is the alternate meant to be consumed. The material cost is duplicate inspection, idle staffed intervals, removable construction, foregone crops and rents, conversion drills, ready stores, and a restoration reserve; margin substitution lets powerful patrons pay to move those costs elsewhere.

## Hooks left

- Does generations-long residence and maintenance ripen into a durable use claim, even if recognizing it makes the margin harder to invoke?
- Which divine patron keeps its own covenant land fixed by financing every usable alternative among people outside its relief rolls?
- After a margin saves the central work and ruins its assigned ward, what evidence distinguishes emergency conversion from risk deliberately sent there from the beginning?

## Rejected first ideas

- Fund rival lift forecasts and compensate idled crews — the prior Ledger explicitly named that seam; writing its safe neighbor would add detail, not possibility.
- Make cult households tithe unused life paths as divine fuel — it confused reachable branches with magical potential and would silently settle unresolved contribution mechanics.
- Establish a salt toll and road-warden market — Kalsa has salt geography but no canon pressure strong enough to keep this from becoming competent generic trade furniture.
