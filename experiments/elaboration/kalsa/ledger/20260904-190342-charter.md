# Charter — 20260904-190342

## Idea

In several Kalsan jurisdictions, the third properly recorded refusal of the same grievance seats a **Court of Returned Seals**: the three refusing authorities have accidentally supplied its mandate and must send a binder, records, and a share of immediate relief. Because Kalsa's powers overlap while each denies owning the whole consequence, the court does not identify the hidden hand; it makes the offices whose routes produced, preserved, or profited from an orphaned act answer materially before they are allowed to use those routes again.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Commoners, workers, dependants, fugitives, and minor offices benefit because three jurisdictional refusals become standing, copied evidence, interim support, and a review clock without requiring proof of a hidden magical author. Offices that profit from divided authority, patrons using friendly acceptance to bury claims, cults laundering intent through intermediaries, and succession factions relying on an unanswerable seal are harmed; legitimate service owners can also be harmed when rivals manufacture returns to quiet necessary authority. The institution costs each participating office an answer share of real material support, binder and clerk labor, duplicate custody, delay, and the risk that minimum-service continuity hands become the next quiet usurpation.
