# Ledger Pass — 20260904-182547

## Idea

Post-Elysium credit is often collateralized by a borrower’s ignorance: a Blind Forecast Covenant seals an oracle’s predicted injuries, defections, and deaths from the people whose choices would make the forecast reflexive. If any named subject learns a protected branch, the forecast—not necessarily the payment—defaults, cascading into repricing, coverage holds, service withdrawal, payroll failure, and route exclusion because every dependent contract was sold against a future that remained bankable only while its participants did not know it.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

- **Benefits:** lenders, insurers, infrastructure patrons, oracle providers, and House Valence gain a financeable risk packet plus the right to reprice when knowledge changes behavior. Managers receive capital by preserving ignorance among people whose decisions the project depends upon.
- **Harmed:** reflexive subjects become economically valuable as uninformed inputs; poor habitats, crews, clinics, workshops, workers, and households cannot afford frequent reforecasting or the liquidity needed to contest contamination. Whistleblowers can be charged for “forecast spoilage” even when a warning prevents the predicted harm.
- **Costs:** each covenant consumes scarce oracle work, observation, surveillance, information partitions, service continuity, and bond liquidity. A knowledge default can freeze credit, alter coverage, shorten supplier terms, pause licensed service, strand movement behind security demands, and spend payroll or maintenance reserves before any office has exceeded its narrow authority.
- **Counter-pressure:** small groups keep unforecast slack—meals, repair time, bunks, clinic transport, instruments, and passage promises allocated by immediate need without requiring the dark schedule’s reason code. It does not save the financial stack; it can make warning or refusal survivable for one person or household.

## Rejected first ideas

- **Pallas consumable vendor-credit choke:** already the explicit next lead from the first Ledger pass and only a safer neighbor to two landed Kappa dependency chains.
- **AU claimshare discount market:** already the second Ledger pass’s named continuation; elaborating acceptors and household exchange would add runtime detail, not a new kind of pressure.
- **Spirits pledged as self-paying collateral:** the vault establishes that spirits consume external energy and fade; “haunted finance” supplied a slogan but no earned institution or non-obvious dependency.
- **Generic self-fulfilling oracle panic:** forecasts changing behavior is already canon. The missing setting concept was the contract that deliberately preserves ignorance, assigns contamination, and makes several independent institutions depend on that ignorance.
