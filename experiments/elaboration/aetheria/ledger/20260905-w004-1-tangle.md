## What life is like

Convoy work brings observations home, and prediction nights turn them into pleasure, reputation and arguments that continue between stations. People can aspire to make discoveries, build instruments, run shops or simply avoid the enthusiasts. Coalition lines cross occupations and ancestry: the camera builder prefers intensive science at the settlement candidate, while Ilan can value a geological result that disappoints his commercial allies.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Instrument workers gain paid work; clubs gain observations; prospective businesses gain a serious assessment; the carrier may eventually gain a useful stop. Subscriptions cost disposable income and studies consume scarce skilled time. Spending on one target delays others, and a negative site result can disappoint people who organized around it. Carrier help changes what is affordable without secretly buying governing authority. Future wet accommodation could constrain a crewed mission, but no quantitative mass penalty or established design is claimed.
