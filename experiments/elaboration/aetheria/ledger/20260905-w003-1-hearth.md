## What life is like

Tonal choice: domestic warmth with a painful, ordinary change in a family relationship. Residents enjoy making distinctive interiors, remembering friends' comfort, and helping with rooms they will not inspect. Sewing and furnishing connect livelihood to private ambition. Adra wants the easy, unplanned presence she shared with Kivet while raising her; Kivet wants comfortable rooms and space to finish work before receiving advice. Neither desire can be met entirely by explaining good manners. Fully accessible households and costly guest rooms remain sincere alternatives.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Different-sized neighbors can receive each other without surrendering every private room to a common body scale. Hosts gain aesthetic and sensory choices. Large visiting rooms consume paid space and heat; partitions require airflow and exits; noise isolation is incomplete. People who associate kinship with wandering freely through a home can experience real loss. Retaining access for a larger relative costs Kivet a sewing room. Smaller-bodied visitors likewise do not gain entitlement to enter private human rooms merely because they fit.
