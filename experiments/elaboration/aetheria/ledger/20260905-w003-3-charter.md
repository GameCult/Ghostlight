## What life is like

People choose lamps and fabrics, mend household things, visit several kitchens, argue about smells, raise children to knock, and make separate rooms compatible with durable love. Competence means making a space fit the person who uses it. Neighbourhood membership can include arrivals; it does not depend on rat-like ancestry. Close company, solitary afternoons, joined households and open doors all have inhabitants who actively prefer them. The tonal blend is domestic warmth with restrained humour and a present political choice.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Shared utilities and small rooms make some privacy affordable. People who need larger spaces cannot participate equally in the existing courts, however friendly the invitations. Construction could let Deren and Mikal share a home and let other unlike bodies settle together; it also ties up money and area that could house more small-bodied residents now. Ivrin's gift is sincere and materially useful without answering Mikal's exclusion from inner domestic life. Wealth already affects how much quiet a resident can buy. No hidden atrocity, employer ultimatum or legal trap supplies the conflict.

The narrow authority claim is local joint ownership of the storeroom and responsibility for its renovation; no species representative, new public office, zoning regime or elaborate voting procedure is proposed. The collective future matters because housing decisions determine which relationships and younger residents can remain in the quarter.
