# Charter Pass — 20260904-184730

## Idea

Many recognized uplift polities are legally required to give an unelected Remainder Seat to whoever guarantees the bodies, scaffolds, and archives of members who do not exist yet. Because suppliers designed those dependencies and can transfer the guarantee through "burden succession," bankruptcies and house inheritances auction a veto over an uplift people's future while calling it protection of their unborn.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

- **Benefits:** uplift suppliers and corporate houses retain a bounded but powerful public office after recognition; insurers and courts gain a solvent counterparty for long-horizon support; later cohorts can genuinely inherit maintained scaffolds, reproductive capacity, archives, and trained care rather than a polity stripped for present creditors.
- **Harmed:** living uplift members can have relocation, emancipation settlements, procurement, reproduction, or dissolution frozen by an unelected holder; communities with proprietary bodies or communication scaffolds are easiest to govern through the future; rival heirs can acquire political voice by acquiring the care burden.
- **Costs:** holders must keep continuance bonds and scheduled support live; communities seeking discharge must duplicate records, spares, clinical capacity, and maintenance knowledge across more than one viable path; every stay consumes time while air, births, credentials, debt, and supplier access continue to run.

## Hooks left

- If a polity chooses not to reproduce, or chooses bodies and scaffolds unlike those it inherited, is that self-determination or actionable waste?
- When a corporate house collapses and different bidders can preserve different pieces of support, can the Seat split without splitting the people whose future it claims to protect?
- If a biodrone line begins reproducing and teaching its own replacements, does a future estate arise where law insists no uplift polity exists?

## Rejected first ideas

- A PSC-wide uplift supreme court — it contradicts the setting's fragmented authority and merely enlarges an existing arbitration layer.
- Ordinary primogeniture rules for a corporate house — succession detail without a new pressure; a runtime elaborator could supply it cheaply.
- A single elected spokesperson for each uplift lineage — the obvious administrative flattening already refused by Corvid and Ratfolk plurality.
- Inheriting a Daedal Figure by proving somatic compatibility — a safe extension of existing envelope lore, not a new institution that changes the surrounding world.
