## What life is like

People grow food, share familiar kitchens, form and end relationships, and keep seeing friends across those changes. They enjoy a meal that can make a former lover and a new lover friends in their own right. Becoming an attentive host is an admired adult ambition. Some people prefer distance, and others want continuity without continuing to provide all its domestic work.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

New partners gain introductions; old friends preserve access to one another; households and children can retain familiar company. Hosts receive affection and esteem. Preparing that welcome takes time and exposes private feelings, and the expectation falls especially heavily on whoever previously organized the group's social life. Adina's attendance would be sincere, but Beren wants the familiar kitchen and labor too. Malek risks having his own first gathering arranged around the old couple's habits.
