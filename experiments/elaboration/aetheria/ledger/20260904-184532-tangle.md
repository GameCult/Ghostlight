# Tangle Pass — 20260904-184532

## Idea

Late-Sol corporations sometimes subsidize genuinely hostile unions, independent habitats, and rival service networks because every documented outsider who depends on them becomes evidence that sanctioning the company would hurt more than the company. The PSC's need to price systemic harm turns opposition into **continuity constituencies**: political collateral that firms must keep independent enough to count, while labor can attack corporate power by coordinating the expensive removal of its own dependencies.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

- **Benefits:** infrastructure firms gain narrower sanctions, protected corridors, higher bonds against attackers, and delay by proving that outsiders depend on them; hostile unions and independent service bodies gain real subsidies and limited bargaining room because their continued separateness has regulatory value; certifiers, insurers, and security contractors gain recurring custody and review work.
- **Harmed:** workers, patients, habitats, and small carriers become political collateral without receiving a vote over the claims made in their name; nominally independent organizations are rewarded for remaining dependent; rivals can target or buy the dependency instead of persuading its people.
- **Costs:** providers must keep adversarial constituencies alive and serviced; dependents must carry parallel systems, tools, records, spares, and receiving capacity to leave; unspooling risks service failure, lost subsidy, exposed maintainers, and replacement by a new patron who wants the same regulatory shield.

## Hooks left

- When does a PSC panel treat an adverse constituency as genuinely independent, and which bloc benefits from exposing a union as a managed shell?
- Can an unspooling campaign remove enough dependencies to weaken a firm without making its workers and habitats cheaper targets for the rivals funding their exit?
- What does a security contractor do when both the provider and the dependent satisfy different release conditions for the only intact hostage file?
