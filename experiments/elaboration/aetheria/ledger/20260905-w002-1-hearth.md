## What life is like

Warm domestic rivalry with room for vanity and mistaken assumptions. People admire timing, accurate shots, and reading a partner. Family attachment survives opposing allegiances in daily acts such as carrying a chair. Team friendships can keep a separated adult socially present. Some residents refuse the intensity and use the shared rooms differently. The ambition is to host visitors and gain a public identity beyond occupational rank; the disagreement concerns which loyalties get displayed.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Nonpilots gain a setting in which their skill and friendships carry standing. Young people can make affiliations their parents did not choose; separated adults can retain relationships beyond a couple. A unified visiting team offers households sincere shared pride. Strong team loyalties can also make a breakup everyone's business, bruise a parent's hopes, or turn an invitation into pressure. Tables and rooms require modest money, space, and upkeep; personal care requires time and willingness. Nothing makes that labor automatically owed or ensures good conduct.
