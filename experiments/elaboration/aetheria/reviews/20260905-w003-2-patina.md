**The strongest ideas**

**Latch Row — aetheria-hearth-20260905-w003-1**

Worth reading because helping furnish a room you cannot enter makes affection concrete. Kivet’s desired home is comfortable for her body and protects her unfinished work; Adra’s loss concerns the spontaneous intimacy of raising someone. Neither is merely failing to communicate.

The Ratfolk owner supports bidirectional access difficulties, sensory variation, and cultural plurality. The candidate respects those distinctions. Morrow Port, its ring, and mixed kinship remain proposed local additions; geographic placement is still for admission. Nothing essential requires an invented capability.

**The Unwatched Sleep — aetheria-numen-20260905-w003-4**

The living sleeper gives mourners something they can actually do. The practice belongs to bedtime, nursing, and familiar boards before it becomes theology. Heth’s desire for Davin’s continuing attention remains painful even after Ulmi offers real help.

The Ratfolk source supports vibration-sensitive embodiment and inherited tectonic danger. Spirits supports conditional persistence with energy costs, not the promised house. The article keeps that boundary intact. Its strongest uncertainty is religious and should remain unresolved.

**The Ruskettle Country — delvehold-tangle-20260905-w003-3**

This offers a place people could inhabit across many stories: seasonal shepherding, quiet companionship, cloth work, fair reunions, marriages, and a town people actively want. The political choice grows from those lives.

Clan streets provide genuine advantages. Independent homes have genuine costs, including displaced grazing. The fleece agreement connects foreign trade to local ambitions without requiring a secretly malicious merchant. Orcs, Humans, and Halflings support the broad social possibilities; the region, ecology, and commercial arrangements are explicitly new. Exact placement remains open.

**The Guest Harvest — kalsa-numen-20260905-w003-4**

An elder tastes evidence that others can manage without them. That is both a pleasure and preparation for death. Isket’s ancestral faith survives her mother’s alternative explanation of the childhood encounter; neither person gets the narrator as a private witness.

Papsenai agriculture supplies the material setting. Death and Binding Law explicitly permits ancestral protection and release traditions while withholding certainty about manifestations. The proposed belief that indispensability hinders departure stays attributed to its adherents. Public presentation would still require a situated source; this shortlist does not authorize a Public rewrite.

This and Unwatched Sleep share a subject, but their actions differ substantially: mourners offer rest after death; living relatives rehearse receiving care before it.

**The Far Hollow — zyphos-numen-20260905-w003-4**

The dead partner’s shelter remains useful, so continued support is neither pointless preservation nor automatically wise. Slant receives news through other bodies. The road wants traffic and nourishment; herds want a good resting place; Ridge wants visitors. Their cooperation does not require identical feelings.

Lantern Trees explicitly establishes mourning signals, mediated information, and sugar exchanges with roads. The candidate proposes their cultural combination rather than presenting it as recovered fact. The interpretation of attachment remains partly observer-owned. The young tree supplies future possibility without inheriting the deceased’s identity.
