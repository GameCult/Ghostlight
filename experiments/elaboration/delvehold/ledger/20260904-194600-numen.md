# Numen Elaboration Ledger

## Idea

The primordial smith is being automated by worship: millions of identical forge-benedictions embedded in standardized spell engines strengthen the god while narrowing it toward the single result their rune graphs demand. Its counter-bargain, the **Second Answer**, grants miracles only to works that reserve one consequential choice for an unrepeatable local maker—including a runic organ or awakened core—so keeping a god a person now requires industrial systems to surrender warranty-grade predictability.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

The primordial smith, the Keepers, local makers, variable runic organisms, and awakened cores benefit from worship that preserves real co-authorship; the Closed Measure, Forge Consortiums, and infrastructure users benefit from the cheaper and highly reliable closed miracles whose repetition creates the danger. Stilling harms gods and saints by preserving their useful effects while erasing their capacity to refuse, reinterpret, or answer anything else, while a live Second Answer can harm households and contract holders when a pump, ward, or brace preserves an admitted purpose by moving cost away from the distribution its owner expected. The bargain costs redundant material, unused capacity, skilled local judgment, warranty coverage, and an enforceable surrender of exact control to the maker present when the work meets real strain.

## Rejected first ideas

- An old core must abandon one ecological function to become a person: strong, but it made awakening itself carry the catastrophe instead of colliding the existing divine and industrial systems.
- A funerary tradition that individuates old cores by cutting away inherited memories: it restated memory disruption and soul pruning in sacramental language.
- Insurers securitize a god's limited attention: the miracle-market collision was invited too directly by the mandate and did not yet force a new account of divine agency.
