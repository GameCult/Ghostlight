# Patina Elaboration Ledger

## Idea

Greathold spell engines traditionally leave one copper seam unsealed so a domesticated mana-eating lichen called **workskin** can drink leaked spell-history and grow it into coloured, rune-shaped bands. The harmless-looking grime is therefore both a safety organ and the only tamper-evident operating record on many pumps and rail engines, so the modern fashion for sealed, self-cleaning housings quietly removes a town's warning system and its evidence at the same time.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Independent rune workshops, technicians, workers, market buyers, and local moots benefit from a safety sink and operating record that remains physically beside the engine; consortium clean-box makers benefit from lower leakage, easier export, mould exclusion, and diagnostics only their service network can read. White-facing harms districts whose early warnings and claim evidence disappear, workers blamed after hidden overdrive, repairers denied a viable mother patch, and the workskin colonies themselves; keeping a skin costs trace leakage, mineral wash, dampness, trimming, skilled reading, slow regrowth, and the risk that neglected lichen bridges a junction.

## Hooks left

- If distant skins grow the same undocumented band before a network anomaly, are they separately recording one shared history or passing a pattern between colonies?
- Who owns a skin pull that proves a public engine was overdriven: the sealed workshop, the workers who kept the colony, the Hold whose pump it was, or the manufacturer whose warranty forbade the witness?
- What must a town do when its workskin begins moving away from safe leakage before the instruments detect anything wrong?

## Rejected first ideas

- Emergency null coins that double as machine parts: the prompt itself offers the wrong-shaped coin, and the landed Null Weather pass already owns distributed null pressure.
- A deliberate maker's flaw that prevents synchronized rune awakening: it is the obvious neighbour of the existing machine-dungeon and the Tangle ledger's rune-standard lead.
- A market in seasoned used conduit: useful texture, but it only extends conductor history and does not create as much pressure as a living safety witness that manufacturers are designing out.
