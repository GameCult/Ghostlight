# Tangle Elaboration Ledger

## Idea

Oathcairn Deep Company has begun paying to awaken selected farm cores, then recognizes them as charter polities with leased chambers, mana stipends, and one carefully bounded power: to consent to extraction through the larger dungeon body around them. By conceding real rights to a dependent few, Oathcairn can present a chorus of dungeon persons demanding that the pumps stay on, fracture claims made in the name of a watershed-scale mind, and turn ecological personhood from the industry's existential threat into its cheapest source of signatures.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Oathcairn, mineral-right Great Houses, Forge Consortiums, commerce-temple insurers, and extraction-dependent Hold districts gain locally legible dungeon allies who can defend supply contracts in hearings and foreign negotiations. The charter cores gain protected territory, stable mana and material, freedom from division and memory reset, and standing denied to most dungeon life; their keepers, some mine unions, and Rune College researchers gain livelihoods and access. Regional cores, watershed-scale claims, displaced goblin communities, unchartered farm strains, and opponents whose evidence describes injury at a larger scale are harmed when the nearest company-fed voice is treated as the only relevant one. The compact costs Oathcairn real concessions: a charter core can veto work inside its chambers, claim a fixed share of returns, demand bounded delves, shelter inhabitants, and mature beyond the preferences it was selected to have; starving, pruning, or dividing it after recognition becomes treaty breach and presumptive murder rather than husbandry.

## Hooks left

- What happens when several charter cores discover that their separate stipends are drawn from one injured regional reserve and form a federation the Near Voice Doctrine was designed to prevent?
- Can a charter core grant asylum to goblins or striking miners inside chambers the company still owns on paper, and which promise controls the next expedition sent through its gate?
- When a charter core withdraws consent but the surrounding deep system appears to demand that delving continue, which scale is the local polity and which is the distant empire?

## Rejected first ideas

- A Rune College accreditation war over proprietary civic standards: the prior Tangle pass named that exact safe next step, and the later Open Work and Workskin passes already tightened the standards-and-warranty ground around it.
- Great Houses reopening failed Holds to revive dormant route claims: the prior Ledger pass explicitly rejected sleeping claims as the safe neighbour of reserve debt and counterseal work.
- A company-backed temple declaring extraction a sacrament: divine legitimation alone would decorate the existing industrial coalition without creating a new political actor or changing who can consent.
