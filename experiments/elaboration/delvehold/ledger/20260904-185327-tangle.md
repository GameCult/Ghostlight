## Idea

The first dungeon polity that Greathold contract law cannot ignore is never admitted as a person: the wounded Silt-Crown watershed quietly buys the bearer debt of the Deep Company drilling into it, paying through goblin factors with treasure deliberately placed in delvers' paths. When Silt-Crown closes the company's galleries and calls the resulting default, the bond lets its temple receiver command drills, payroll, and rail allotments, forcing every interested power to choose between dishonouring debt and admitting that a landscape has executed a hostile takeover.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Silt-Crown gains a legal hand inside the extraction system; its goblin factors gain commissions and a client powerful enough to defend their route knowledge; mineworkers gain wages, withdrawal thresholds, and a temporary alliance against Seven Weirs management; the Open Scale and senior bearers preserve the credibility of their covenants. Seven Weirs' owners lose operational control, junior bondholders lose interest, dependent Holds and foreign pump towns absorb rationing, and neighbouring dungeon organs may receive work displaced from Silt-Crown; the takeover costs the core old treasure, ecological reserves, and continued complicity in enough extraction to keep the company solvent.

## Hooks left

- Can a creditor cause default by refusing injury to its own body, and which boundary of a nested watershed core decides that question?
- Before the plates mature in eight years, can Silt-Crown turn receivership into a treaty or civic seal without making the temple its permanent legal sovereign?

## Rejected first ideas

- Rune-standard accreditation as a political cartel: the prior Tangle pass already left that exact pressure as its next lead.
- A watershed that enforces contract patterns carried in mana: this was a safe neighbour of the landed Null Weather idea, changing the payload while repeating the mechanism.
- An ascended god born from a public bond: the prior Numen lead already points at divine debt, and the idea would make theology rather than political relationship the owner.
- Foreign embassies founded as Greathold workshops: it follows the open franchise, but without a sharper inversion it remains runtime-grade institutional detail.
