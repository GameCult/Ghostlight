# Numen elaboration ledger

## Idea

The primordial smith has begun granting miracles to dungeons: creatures, fungal colonies, and living stone exposed to industrial mana flows are evolving runic organs that perform the old repair-and-return liturgy more faithfully than most modern factories do. Because the god's bargain answers completed work rather than ancestry or declared belief, the Greathold is breeding a rival priesthood inside the ecology it wounds, and an old core can now speak to a celestial god by composing its species into a rite.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

The primordial smith gains congregations able to preserve, test, and repair work across centuries; damaged dungeons gain bounded repairs, a divine channel, and a way for an awakened core to make intention legible without adopting mortal speech. Forge priests who admit the phenomenon gain new miracles and ecological authority, while Rune Colleges gain living evidence that repeated standards become heritable magical pressure. Deep Companies, core farmers, proprietary rune houses, and industrial theologians lose exclusive control of craft, doctrine, and spell forms; mine districts can also be crushed or flooded when a blessed repair serves the dungeon's boundary rather than the settlement inside it. Every rite spends mana and bodily matter, must test a real work to failure, and must return useful substance to its source; the blessing remembers only the tested failure and offers no general invulnerability.

## Hooks left

- When an awakened core deliberately omits the final return, is it refusing the primordial smith, bargaining for different terms, or warning mortals that the god's bargain is itself extractive?
- If a proprietary military rune appears as an organ in a distant dungeon connected by deep mana currents, is the organism stolen property, an uncontrolled weapons bearer, a revelation, or evidence that the standard already injured a larger body?
- Can the emerging [[Machine-Dungeon|machine-dungeon]] perform the rite as one distributed worshiper, and what counts as “returning useful substance to its source” when the worshiper is simultaneously factory, city, mountain tissue, and population?

## Rejected first ideas

- A commerce god placing retroactive liens on crystals after a core countersigns its extraction contract: the divine accounting was sharp, but the countersignature repeated the prior Answering Cut's core-recognition mechanism and followed that pass's explicit next lead too safely.
- A temple rite that declares a mature core a saint or legal person: this was the Answering Cut with a different officiant, exactly the neighbor the swarm must not build.
- Proprietary rune houses prosecuting animals whose organs copy industrial spell graphs: the ownership problem survives as a hook, but on its own it is a patent dispute with antlers rather than a numinous pressure.
