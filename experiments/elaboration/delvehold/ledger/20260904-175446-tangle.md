## Elaboration

Expanded the reserve houses already named in `Dwarven Industry` into a political institution with a concrete mandate, constituency, dependencies, and coercive limits. Their factors hold pooled crystal, parts, credit, and pledged capacity; infrastructure webs depend on them for ordinary continuity, while reserve houses depend on moot levies, repayments, company supply, consortium standards, labour cooperation, and public legitimacy. A factor may call a loan, suspend insurance, or refuse stock, but cannot seize an infrastructure seal or purchase moot votes. The dangerous bargain is an emergency release that keeps service alive now while binding later standards, inspection, revenue, and repair access.
