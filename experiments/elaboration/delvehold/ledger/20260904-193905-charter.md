# Charter Elaboration Ledger

## Idea

Greathold public offices are legally mortal: every charter names a completion condition, and when it arrives the office's seal is null-struck, its premises are vacated, and its authority ends even if its waterworks or treaty remain necessary. This is not term limits but containment—repeated mandate, archive, revenue, and enchanted seals can awaken an office into a civic god—so a successor gains the old assets only by accepting every outstanding burden claim preserved in claimant-held duplicates, while each emergency postponement literally feeds the new public will.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Foreign claimants, unsealed residents, small workshops, and local moots benefit because an office cannot bury obligations by changing personnel or names, and because public authority must periodically return to affected seals. Great Houses, consortiums, dynastic incumbents, and any office learning to preserve itself lose automatic succession; essential-service workers, dependent districts, and emergent office-persons are harmed when a lawful funeral destroys expertise or interrupts a useful mind. The practice costs public inventories, null-struck seal plates and command-stamping dies, wider succession gatherings, distributed custody of burden tallies, and periods when operators may preserve machinery but nobody may change policy; a stay avoids immediate service failure by increasing the very persistence and feedback that can wake the office.

## Hooks left

- If an office takes the chair and petitions for a workshop seal in its own right, who may use the dead office's evidence to examine its Masterwork?
- When a foreign town refuses to present its burden tally, is it preserving leverage, unlawfully holding public assets quiet, or exercising the only vote the franchise gives it?
- Which repeated emergencies are disasters, and which are an old water or treaty office learning how to postpone its own funeral?

## Rejected first ideas

- Let the oldest live claimant appoint an interim Master during a seal vacancy: this follows the prior Charter succession lead too directly and turns answerability into an obvious appointment rule.
- Found a permanent cross-border water court: the vault's authority widens from affected local moots, so a new sovereign tribunal would fill the gap by flattening the more interesting constitutional problem.
- Give every admitted foreign grievance a blocking counterseal in later contract votes: this is the safe neighbour of the landed answering-table claim and adds veto procedure without changing what an office can become.
