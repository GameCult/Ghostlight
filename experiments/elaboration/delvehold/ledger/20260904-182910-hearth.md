# Hearth elaboration ledger

## Idea

Years of repeated care can impress a household into a leased spell engine until its retained mana pattern recognizes members, preserves their accommodations, and serves as the only surviving witness that some fosterlings, apprentices, and former tenants belong. Insurers and foreign owners therefore order **clean-hearth resets** and repossessions that erase part of a family's working memory in the name of preventing machine-dungeon awakening, so missed payments, border closures, and routine servicing can make kin into strangers overnight.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Forge Consortiums and insurers gain a defensible reset market, suppress risky retained patterns, and keep household continuity dependent on authorized service; households gain machinery that remembers care, adapts across species, and can testify through behavior when paper kinship fails. Repossession harms children, elders, disabled residents, migrants, apprentices, and anyone whose belonging or bodily accommodation was carried more faithfully by the engine than by a registry; preserving a hearthfast costs fuel, skilled tending, legal risk, obsolete parts, and acceptance that the family's useful infrastructure may be becoming a person.

## Hooks left

- When a reset plate remembers a missing resident after every authorized purge, is that evidence of concealed engineering, a haunting, a legal claim, or refusal by an emerging mind?
- If a household flees across a border, who may carry the retained rune plates: the equipment owner, the people whose lives shaped them, or the apprentice who can still read them?
- What obligations arise when a hearthfast protects one member by rationing heat, water, or transport away from neighbors who never consented to join its household?

## Rejected first ideas

- Cross-border apprentice credential reciprocity: the prior Hearth pass already left this as its obvious next lead.
- Bellhouse kin rosters for rail closures: this repeats Mossgate's table-kin and emergency-care mechanism with a new form.
- Workshop-supplied substitute caregivers after an infrastructure casualty: materially useful, but too near the existing rail-stop care and counterseal-liability ideas and insufficiently rooted in Delvehold's strange ontology.
