## Idea

The first mana storm to breach the Greathold does not overload a single rune: it carries the null casting rune through the civic feed, and every connected spell engine shuts itself down correctly. The null pattern is industrial waste remembered by deep mana currents, so the safety stroke that made mass automation possible has become a planetary immune response capable of stopping pumps, wards, natural runic organs, and restart attempts without leaving a broken machine for insurers to blame.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Deep Companies benefit from the quiet cut, when stillfall-disabled dungeon defences expose reserves, and insurers benefit when an orderly null state lets them deny machinery-breakdown claims. Cistern districts, critical-service workers, dependent Holds, and organisms whose runic organs sustain life bear the immediate harm; survival costs sealed clean-start crystal, physical isolation from the shared feed, skilled manual operation, flooded-entry delves, abandoned services, and a stronger later stillfall when companies exploit the ecological paralysis.
