# Ledger elaboration ledger

## Idea

The Greathold has learned that an industrial network becoming a dungeon must itself be delved: without genuine hostile intrusion, its spell engines hoard patterned mana, over-specialize their safeguards, and eventually fail together like an undelved core. Insurers therefore price and trade a Hold's **break burden**—the required exposure of live connected machinery to licensed delvers who may actually damage it and keep what they extract—so rich districts buy continuity by paying poorer Holds to host the dangerous contests their shared machine needs.

## Who benefits / who is harmed / what it costs

Insurers and lenders gain a measurable condition for covering connected infrastructure; wealthy Holds buy uninterrupted services; Great Houses rent old galleries; Forge Consortiums sell burden-rated engines and replacements; delver halls recover paid work inside the industrial economy. Poor and frontier Holds receive machinery, crystal priority, wages, and salvage at the price of becoming the contest ground: residents without seals endure isolation, workers prepare functioning systems for legal attack, parties risk death, and a successful delve may remove the component the host needs next winter. A valid discharge costs real load-bearing machinery, outages, released mana, lost tools, injury, redundancy, and the political right to expose a living district; a tame or refused discharge leaves the shared network vulnerable to correlated failure.

## Hooks left

- If a burden gallery opens a path to evidence of fraud instead of valuable machinery, is the network offering salvage, giving testimony, or directing an attack?
- Can a Hold that has sold its break burden later refuse the delve when residents, rather than sealed workshops, will bear the outage?
- What happens when the Deep World's response learns that violence in a burden gallery is being used to preserve the extraction network beyond it?

## Rejected first ideas

- A toll regime for mana moving between Holds: the prior Ledger pass already made conduit capacity, tax situs, and remote service dependence its central mechanism.
- Rune-standard warranties that lock municipal technicians to consortium parts: the Tangle ledger already names that exact safe next seam, while ecological attacks on standards are established canon.
- Insurers taking future cistern output as collateral after a failure: this repeats the Glasswater lease and Brasswater reserve-call territory with a different form.
