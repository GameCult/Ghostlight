**The strongest ideas**

- **The Long Mouth — aetheria-patina-20260905-w002-3.** Pell is not merely resisting innovation: his partner has begun inviting an audience to laugh down at something he is still performing sincerely. Sera actually likes the new work. Private bookings and the anniversary couple prevent either artistic fashion from owning the meaning of the old act. This is worth reading because embarrassment changes a relationship before anyone formally ends it.

  The Pallas strikes, BioElevate, and Syndicate sources support the labor history and dry-operation equipment. The proposal accommodates that equipment rather than silently supplying an aquatic sanctuary. Remaining uncertainty concerns the new venue and performers’ particular working arrangements, not a contradictory capability.

- **Understep House Plays — delvehold-hearth-20260905-w002-2.** The children hiding their new clothes are the decisive detail. A successful joke has become inaccurate, and the people who pay for that inaccuracy are not its original collaborators. The invented-household genre also offers an artistic future beyond correcting Vessa’s manners.

  `Goblins.md` supports the settlement’s habitat and livelihoods; `Monsters.md` supports its theatrical subjects. Speaking fungi and spiders remain fiction within the fiction. Understep’s precise location and visiting audience are ordinary proposed additions. No dungeon response or species-wide comic temperament is needed.

- **Prayers in Another Mouth — kalsa-numen-20260905-w002-1.** The practice has an intelligible religious attraction before it becomes a dispute. Tal helped Mera reach the shrine; his refusal therefore cannot be dismissed as indifference to her survival. Mera wants her gratitude spoken accurately, which cannot be satisfied by thanking somebody else more tactfully.

  `Null and Control Descendants.md` supports plural monotheistic developments and mixed ancestry. `God-Cult Covenants.md` distinguishes worship, membership, and binding. The candidate preserves those distinctions: the reciter’s answer is theology, not author-certified metaphysics. The One Listener’s reality and any magical consequences remain unresolved. That uncertainty belongs in the story.

- **The Unanswered Scraps — zyphos-veil-20260905-w002-1.** The best discovery is that the puzzle does not establish what its maker thought it established. This gives the colonies an enjoyment that follows from their bodies and evidence media, rather than putting miniature human detectives into ant costumes. Fading glue also makes delayed disclosure consequential without inventing an archive office.

  `Lattice Ants.md` already grants colony-scale investigation, deception, negotiated reporting, and physically assembled intelligence. Recreation and recognition of alternative sequences are substantive new behavioral proposals, but compatible ones; I accept them for shortlist consideration as written. Neither individual ants nor microbial glue acquire independent reflective personhood.

- **Turn Mantles — zyphos-hearth-20260905-w002-2.** Preparing an appearance for one watching branch makes attraction spatial and bodily. The quiet mantle then lets someone change their taste without requiring a political awakening or a concealed rebellion. The maker-credit hook is especially good: supplying the cloth and making the visible choices are different contributions.

  `Airawa.md` supports simultaneous anchoring, bracing, manipulation, and socially readable clothing. `Airawa Empire.md` permits productive initiative while withholding independent political purposes; the engine source reaches associations and desires. The candidate retains those constraints. Employer tolerance and garment arrangements remain local additions, not an engine loophole.
