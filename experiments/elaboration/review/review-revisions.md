## Revision 2 — Aetheria

Operator corrections:

Its limits rule out treating the Dominion as omniscient or every association as automatically punishable.

### A1 — replaced mechanism

Removed the claim that a short review interval justifies sustained estrangement. Removed compulsory missed celebrations, implied affection-detection as the main obstacle, and the unsupported attribution to GeneSys inheritance law.

The new proposal gives dependants a continuing placement under an independent sponsor's housing and care entitlement. Independence is assessed through financial support and recurring care. The sacrifice is keeping the spare home and declining shared obligations; staged dislike does not qualify anyone. The receiving household's contract provides the protection, and ordinary clearance review of the former household alone supplies no ground to cancel it.

This is proposed institutional design, not newly discovered source canon. Eligibility depends on actual housing and compatible care, and a separate security or custody order remains effective. The operator has not yet accepted this mechanism.

### A2 — changed entry point

Added When the Nursery Changes Hands near the top: familiar staff leave, facilities consolidate, charges grow, and children's developmental choices shrink. The legal explanation follows the parents' failed attempt to replace the provider.

Clarified the difference between the charter's minimum guarantee and the fuller service families depended on. An acquirer can satisfy the former while degrading the latter. This supplies a reason the bond does not immediately defeat the acquisition strategy. The Seat and control of the needed assets make exit a political/legal struggle rather than a simple change of supplier.
