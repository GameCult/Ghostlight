# The Far Hollow

*Canon candidate. Locality and translated names are provisional.*

At the Far Hollow, glassback calves rest beneath a stone overhang whose lantern tree has died. Amber fungal candles still mark the approach. A living tree farther uphill supplies sugars to the road that keeps those candles open, although the calves shelter beyond the reach of its roots. Local route readers understand this continued care as mourning: the uphill tree keeps asking for news of a place it once shared with another living being.

The hollow lies on an Umbros-facing slope of the [[World/Sa'ueia Continent|Sa'ueia continent]], before imperial contact. A small community of [[Umbros-Facing Lantern Trees]], fungal routes, couriers and returning herds lives around its wetland edge. Mats provide feeding nearby; the overhang shelters resting animals. The lost tree shaded the approach and guided arrivals until a windthrow killed it. The stone roof survived.

The uphill tree is provisionally called Slant. While its neighbour lived, both supplied the same [[Candle Fungal Roads|candle fungal road]]. Reports passing through that partnership made the hollow familiar to Slant. Lantern trees receive root pressure, fungal reports and the chemicals of distressed infants; their records do not require them to look down upon a scene as a walking visitor would. Slant knew this place largely through what arrived from it.

Now the familiar reports arrive without the neighbour's contributions. Slant continues supplying the hollow's road branch and displays mourning pulses when couriers return from there. Among the local trees, this has become one possible way to answer a partner's death: support a place the partner made habitable, and keep receiving news of its present use.

## What Returns

For the [[Glassback Grazers|glassbacks]], the hollow offers a familiar resting stop between feeding and onward travel. Adults settle flank-to-flank during bright recovery, passing warmth and low-grade route confidence. Calves learn the approach by following a herd that comes willingly. The living congregation, if a visitor wants to call it that, mostly wants to be warm.

[[Threadwing Couriers|Threadwings]] enjoy the dependable route and payment at Slant's grove. Some make a particular habit of returning from the hollow before taking other work. They bring current route memories in the living cargo carried within their guts. The fungal road also reports conditions from below. Slant repeatedly renews the sugar arrangement after these returns. Readers take that recurrence as evidence that hearing about the hollow matters to it; nobody can extract a complete account of what satisfaction feels like inside its trunk.

The road has its own reasons to cooperate. Herd dung feeds it, traffic carries spores, and Slant remains a useful producer partner. It can keep the hollow viable without sharing Slant's attachment. Its reports include hunger, emptiness and damage. A comforting report invented for the occasion would make it a poor partner.

## A Place Still Taking Visitors

A neighbouring lantern tree, called Ridge, offers good shelter closer to the feeding mats. It admits the herds and rewards couriers who direct traffic there. Several returning herds already choose that stop. Ridge's preference is legible through these invitations: it wants the living activity beneath its own canopy. It has no obligation to preserve another tree's favoured arrangement.

Slant also needs pollination, healthy roots and reserves. Keeping the remote branch supplied leaves less sugar for enlarging its own courier service. Some route readers admire the persistence; others think their reliable reports are helping Slant continue an attachment it would otherwise revise. Couriers who favour the hollow can still prefer a better-paid route when nesting demands increase.

The practice has no promised duration. The records held in [[Ecology/Mutable Memory Endosymbiosis|living memory]] do not establish that the dead tree survives in Slant. What remains open is how long this particular relationship with a place will keep changing the survivor's choices.

## Hooks Left

- The road offers to reduce the hollow branch while keeping it passable. Slant must answer through the next sugar exchange; the couriers have already begun planning around those supplies.
- A herd raised on the hollow route takes its calves to Ridge. Its familiar traces reach Slant through the road, carrying news of a successful rest somewhere else.
- A young lantern tree begins growing beside the overhang. Slant's couriers must decide whether their next delivery should pass it by or land there first.
