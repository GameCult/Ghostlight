## What deserves to survive

**Delvehold has the strongest connected portfolio.** The industrial ecology premise yields different kinds of consequence rather than merely different terminology. Silt-Crown converts dungeon treasure and delving into a hostile takeover; the receiver must keep extracting to service the debt that gives the watershed control. That second trap makes the idea work. Workskin gives mechanics something to touch, read, trade, neglect, and lose. The Second Answer turns industrial standardization into danger to a god's personhood. Together these can support adventures, household conflict, theology, and economic history.

**Aetheria has the strongest intimate premise: Keeping The Quarrel.** The absent relative is not just a workaround for surveillance. They miss births and funerals to remain useful; protection consumes the separation; a protective fiction can become actual abandonment. The 17-line addition carries more human consequence than several much longer procedural notes. Its legal opening also has an intelligible reason to persist: elite inheritance networks benefit from limits on associated liability.

**Kalsa's best work makes divinity change information politics.** The Cinder Concord follows the existing bounded divine knowledge model: what a witness remembers may become available to a hierarchy. Fragmentation, reconstruction, and deception therefore have distinctly Kalsan stakes. The text remembers that a distributed accusation can still be unjust, and that smoke-bearers pay for other people's secrecy. The Open Margin and Closed Hour are a promising opposed pair: preserving alternatives versus deliberately exhausting them.

**Zyphos succeeds when biology performs the politics.** Counterroot Embassies make diplomacy depend on immune pruning, reproductive resin, waste, and archive tissue; the brood has interests of its own. Benefactor Capture is especially economical: useful gifts condition bodies until alternatives become dangerous. Neither idea needs an administrative office to become consequential.

## Where editorial judgment must intervene

### 1. Rediscovery is already happening

Zyphos Hearth 190604 produces Future-Kin; Hearth 195915 produces Counterbrood Kinship. Both externalize a child's bodily futures into multiple cross-species carriers, make handover physiological, and make lost relationships narrow possible adulthood. They are not identical: the former emphasizes required developmental patterns and redundant agreement; the latter emphasizes unused repair/adaptation alternatives. They nevertheless occupy substantially the same conceptual territory. Integrate one mechanism, preserving the useful distinction if desired, rather than admitting two independently specified biological systems.

The timing supplies a concrete cause. The first pass finished at 20:03:41; the second launched at 19:59:15. The second ledger's reading list does not include the first. Workers clone completed ledgers once at launch and share neither active ideas nor the other workers' proposed lore. This is missing context, not evidence that an elaborator ignored visible work.

There are further overlaps to compare, not automatically delete: Hunger Saints/Tally Ghosts; Counterroot Embassies/Two-Crown Hunger; Last-Return Rights/Contradiction Quorums; and the cross-world Mortal Charters/Burial of Offices pair.

### 2. Different worlds keep discovering similar institutions

Delvehold and Kalsa both produce office funerals, terminated seals, surviving burdens, and restrictions on successors inheriting assets without liabilities. Delvehold adds literal institutional awakening; Kalsa explicitly rejects that reading and supplies civic procedure. These distinctions matter, but the repeated central invention reduces portfolio breadth.

Across worlds, the recurring vocabulary is witness, archive, dependency, custody, collateral, and authority. This is partly requested by the briefs and partly supported by the source settings. It is not automatically a defect. It becomes one when eight mandates repeatedly produce administrative mechanisms rather than eight different ways of encountering a world.

The prompts themselves invite this convergence: law with a clock, debt, contract forms, insurance, gods charging interest. Patina's low-stakes mandate also competes with a global demand that every idea surprise the author and be causally load-bearing. Workskin navigates that tension well, but increasing Patina frequency alone cannot guarantee ordinary texture.

### 3. Novelty sometimes spends foundational uncertainty

The Second Answer is excellent material, but the parent Gods and Religion note says the relation of worship to divine generation remains unresolved. The new note opens by asserting that worship tells a god what it is and defines stilling as a real condition. That is a significant epistemic commitment, even if it can be made compatible with the older ontology. Admit it deliberately or retain competing explanations around observed effects.

The Unwritten Root installs a universal authorization dependency at the center of the Biological Memetic Engine. The old engine note establishes propagation, engineered topology, and path dependency; it does not establish a requirement that universal doctrine receive independent biological attestation. The new organ is a plausible invention, not a necessary deduction. It also strongly resembles an engineering solution to self-authentication. Decide whether this is wanted founding history before inserting it as something the empire has always required.

The Black Margin is distinctive and bounded, but adds another institutional pattern becoming divine. Consider it beside Mortal Charters and The Second Answer, not independently. Repeatedly making metaphors literally alive can quietly settle the setting's largest mysteries.

### 4. Summary strength and prose strength differ

Silt-Crown and Keeping The Quarrel gain force in their full additions: named stakes, material actions, costs, and people with incompatible interests. The Burial of Offices expands to 188 added lines, many devoted to hearings, inventories, owner boundaries, and continuity. That can be useful reference material, but much of it develops a procedure after the central invention is already clear. It risks sounding like the engineering manual that generated it.

Do not solve this with a universal word cap. Preserve each concept's operative mechanism and remove repetitive declarations of ownership, limitations, and redress when the surrounding vault already supplies them. Allow mystery and ordinary life to remain where exhaustive procedure adds little.

### 5. Proposal status is blurred in the worker context

The handoff correctly calls the additions canon candidates and reserves integration for later judgment. The prompt calls them setting canon and describes prior ledger ideas as already landed. Kalsa additions include `status: canonical-institution`, and the Cinder Concord adds an `adopted_in` stamp naming its own pass. Branch isolation currently prevents automatic admission to main, but a later reader can mistake proposal prose for approval.

Current authority map: the source baseline owns established canon; workers own proposals; the scheduler owns transport and publication of branches/ledgers; the operator or designated editor owns admission; aggregate ledgers index proposed work. A ledger summary does not make its assumptions canon, and a successful push proves neither quality nor approval.

## Before another run

1. Make editorial selections now. Select a small set per world, identify dependencies, and explicitly decide foundational claims. Do not merge branches solely because text conflicts resolve.
2. Give the next cohort the actual admitted lore and clearly labeled remaining proposals. Preserve rejection reasons; do not force workers to rediscover the same editorial boundaries.
3. Reduce overlapping invention until active proposals are visible. A small sequential concept pass per world is the simplest comparison run; larger concurrency must buy something beyond more simultaneous guesses. This is a proposed experiment, not a measured optimum.
4. Evaluate the next cohort by distinct mechanisms, setting specificity, story choices, and integration cost. Title mix is an input, not a measure of tonal success.
5. Keep the good hooks unresolved. Full stories and fixtures are not necessary to evaluate concept yield, but a concept should enable concrete action, conflicting desires, or a discovery—not merely another audit.

The frequency-only tone claim also needs clarification before restart. The recorded operator directive says tone is set by title frequency alone; the current renderer injects Tone and Lens prose directly. Preserve the directive and resolve the mismatch deliberately.
