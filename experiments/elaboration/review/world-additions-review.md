## Changes worth noticing

**A1 is demoted.** The operator judged the revised practice too mundane and inconsequential to warrant an addition. Its draft remains in the Aetheria file as review history; it is excluded from integration.

**A2 now starts with parents living through an acquisition.** The care team leaves, local provision closes, and developmental services become extras. The buyer's Seat can block moving the facilities to a replacement provider. The draft then explains why minimum guarantees leave room for profitable deterioration and how the Seat follows the care obligation through a sale.

**Future-Kin and Counterbrood Kinship become one proposal.** Future-kin are the hosts; counterbroods are the cultures they carry. The cultures can support maturation or later repair. The original fixed carrier quorum and three-eclipse transfer rule are removed. Required maturation and preserved alternative development are no longer two unrelated physiological systems. One new Ecology note would own the mechanism, with short links from family life and breeding grounds.

**Delvehold's knowledge boundary is explicit.** Dungeon sentience is a contentious fringe academic debate; industrial awakening is even more fringe. D1's creditors are goblin traders with unusual private expedition records. No court recognizes a dungeon person. D2 contains no awakening debate, and D3 no longer assumes cores participate in accepted religious rites.

**Workskin is an inherited craft practice.** Artisans share local strains and teach apprentices how to tend and interpret them. Sleek sealed products remove the space for the growth, and their designers dismiss or overlook the practice. Formal skin records and diagnostic standardization have been removed. Forge Consortiums already has an owner section in Dwarven Industry; the relevant mention links there rather than creating a duplicate institution.

**The Second Answer uses the agreed relationship between gods and worship.** Veyra needs worship and knows that its form shapes her. Her domain limits what she can do; what a blessing teaches believers affects whether she chooses to give it. Her high priests and paladins understand this and curate worship in close relationship with her. Industrial patronage brings resources and pressure over what the temple celebrates. The proposed second hearing gives people affected by a bridge a place in that worship. Copied miracles and machine operation as worship have been removed.

**The Cinder Concord cannot undo an earlier disclosure.** A cutter initially knows the whole record. Dispersal protects against later capture and accumulation; it does not erase divine memory. The proposed text preserves that vulnerability rather than inventing a new concealment power.

**The Open Margin and Closed Hour share constraints, not an institution.** They are reviewed together but retain distinct owner notes: the first is a public-work practice; the second a conspiracy. Existing Kaos and Oracle Ecology notes continue owning the causal rules.

**Remainder Charters are jurisdictional.** The draft does not make all uplift recognition depend on a supplier's Seat. It keeps the Portia hearings as an unresolved precedent later jurists exploit.

**Procedural expansion was cut selectively.** Witness risk, challenged evidence, insolvency, bodily refusal, and continuity costs remain. Long inventories and parallel statements about each office's boundaries were reduced. Every entry retains open consequences for later work.
