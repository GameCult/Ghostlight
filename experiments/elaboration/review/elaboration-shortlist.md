# Elaborator shortlist

Reviewer-promoted candidates from the new campaign. These remain proposals for your review; none has been merged into canon. Each wave compares sixteen ideas.

## Wave 001

- **Aetheria — [Longbank Clay Walks](candidates/aetheria-hearth-20260905-w001-1.md)**

  An Elephant Enclave neighborhood makes communal clay landscapes that children, elders, and friends explore and reshape together. Beloved works accumulate family attachments, leaving residents to choose between preserving shared places and giving new makers room.

- **Delvehold — [The Second Learners of Bracken Fold](candidates/delvehold-hearth-20260905-w001-1.md)**

  Bracken Fold is an orcish upland community whose households share work so adults and elders can spend a winter learning something new. Its families must decide how far to support a loved one's growing ambition when further learning may take them away from the people who made it possible.

- **Delvehold — [Lethmere Shorefolk](candidates/delvehold-patina-20260905-w001-4.md)**

  The Lethmere shorefolk spend the dry season in separate lakeside homes and the rains together in large hillside houses, carrying their doors between them. Their growing wish for permanent private homes tests a seasonal intimacy that many still enjoy and rely on.

- **Kalsa — [The Waking Houses of Clayfold](candidates/kalsa-hearth-20260905-w001-1.md)**

  A small Spoker-descended pottery settlement makes kinship through neighbouring households whose waking hours overlap, so children and sick people can find familiar company while their own household sleeps. Courtship and starting a private home become consequential choices because changing when one is awake changes whom one can care for.

- **Zyphos — [The Near Flights](candidates/zyphos-hearth-20260905-w001-1.md)**

  A threadwing roost community maintains short companion flights for adjusting to regrowing sensory vanes and enjoying one another's company. Its members must choose how to sustain those attachments while pursuing routes that may carry them far apart.

## Wave 002

- **Aetheria — [The Long Mouth](candidates/aetheria-patina-20260905-w002-3.md)**

  A Pallas cephalopod shadow theatre has made its reputation on elaborate comic creatures, but audiences are beginning to prefer sparse, hesitant performances. Two established partners must choose whether to keep playing their beloved jaw act sincerely when one has started sharing the fashionable audience's embarrassment.

- **Delvehold — [Understep House Plays](candidates/delvehold-hearth-20260905-w002-2.md)**

  In one goblin neighbourhood, short domestic farces turn the habits of loved people into recurring stage characters. A successful impersonation can outlast the relationship or change of heart that made it affectionate, forcing performers to choose what they are willing to notice about their subjects.

- **Kalsa — [Prayers in Another Mouth](candidates/kalsa-numen-20260905-w002-1.md)**

  One local Control-descended monotheistic community exchanges prayers by speaking another person's wishes in the first person. A recovered woman's thanksgiving to a neighbouring god tests whether her husband can lend his voice without lending his allegiance.

- **Zyphos — [The Unanswered Scraps](candidates/zyphos-veil-20260905-w002-1.md)**

  A small community of lattice-ant colonies exchanges staged, incomplete traces for the pleasure of reconstructing what happened. Their disagreement over when to reveal an answer becomes consequential when a courier mistakes demonstrated uncertainty for extraordinary forensic power.

- **Zyphos — [Turn Mantles](candidates/zyphos-hearth-20260905-w002-2.md)**

  In one imperial Airawa clothing neighborhood, people enjoy mantles whose patterns appear in sequence as their six-limbed wearers climb and adjust folds. A change in someone's taste can unsettle an intimate collaboration even when both people sincerely value their imperial lives.

## Wave 003

- **Aetheria — [Latch Row](candidates/aetheria-hearth-20260905-w003-1.md)**

  A mixed Ratfolk–human neighborhood builds generous visiting rooms in front of private interiors that need not admit every loved one's body. Helping someone make a home one cannot enter becomes a familiar act of affection, while families disagree over how much physical access intimacy needs.

- **Aetheria — [The Unwatched Sleep](candidates/aetheria-numen-20260905-w003-4.md)**

  One Ratfolk community gives mourners a shared sleep while praying that the dead have reached a home where nobody needs them to keep watch. A bereaved parent's hope that her partner still protects their child conflicts with her sister's hope that he can finally rest.

- **Delvehold — [The Ruskettle Country](candidates/delvehold-tangle-20260905-w003-3.md)**

  The Ruskettle country is a proposed orcish wool-producing region whose families want their seasonal market to grow into a town with livelihoods for their children. Clan-funded streets and independently owned homes attract competing alliances because both promise a desirable future while giving relatives different powers over one another.

- **Kalsa — [The Guest Harvest](candidates/kalsa-numen-20260905-w003-4.md)**

  A small papsenai farming community feeds elders from a crop raised without their assistance, letting them experience being cared for before death. Participants disagree about whether this prepares the dead to leave their descendants or to remain as more generous ancestors.

- **Zyphos — [The Far Hollow](candidates/zyphos-numen-20260905-w003-4.md)**

  A lantern tree continues supporting a shelter once maintained by a dead neighbour, receiving news through a fungal road and returning couriers because its own roots cannot reach the place. Its attachment sustains a useful stop while other trees, herds and couriers increasingly prefer different arrangements.

## Wave 004

- **Aetheria — [The Turnbooks Of Mere Landing](candidates/aetheria-patina-20260905-w004-4.md)**

  At a small post-Elysium repair landing, people make folding picture books that accompany an ordinary walk past views of unfamiliar spatial geometry. Their growing popularity gives artists a choice between showing a destination's surprise in advance and composing the experience of discovering it on foot.

- **Delvehold — [The Keshet Saltfolk](candidates/delvehold-ledger-20260905-w004-2.md)**

  The Keshet saltfolk buy their food with salt, and a proposed cart road could let more pan workers carry their own harvest. Its new alignment would bypass two villages whose trade and friendships help carrier families survive bad seasons.  Revised from: delvehold-ledger-20260905-w003-4, branch slot/delvehold/ledger/20260905-w003-4.

- **Delvehold — [The Rain Music of Tovren](candidates/delvehold-numen-20260905-w004-4.md)**

  An orcish pottery town offers rain music to a storm goddess and enjoys the weather interrupting its carefully learned rhythms. Its musicians disagree over whether worship should welcome divine wildness or praise the goddess for choosing gentleness, as their songs begin travelling beyond the town.

- **Kalsa — [The Bowl Asks After You](candidates/kalsa-patina-20260905-w004-2.md)**

  At Reed Elbow, friends invent voices for household possessions that carry their shared jokes into later meals. Udrem's carefully divided food and a nephew's wish to inherit Chavi's bowl test whether a cherished friendship can make room for new pleasures and new participants.  Revised from: kalsa-patina-20260905-w003-2, branch slot/kalsa/patina/20260905-w003-2.

- **Kalsa — [The Sling Yard](candidates/kalsa-ledger-20260905-w004-4.md)**

  A provincial sling yard teaches the same material craft to people who experience its loaded joints differently through Channeling and Analysis. Two partners become profitable together before either has learned what they hoped to learn from the other, making practice time and good cord a contested investment.
